Spendly was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: make expense tracking effortless for Indians. We understand the unique financial habits of Indian households - from monthly budgets to festival expenses, from UPI payments to cash transactions.

Today, we're proud to serve over 50,000+ users across India, helping them save crores of rupees through smart expense tracking.
