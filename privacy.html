Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, add expenses, or contact us for support. This may include: name, email address, financial information, usage data, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to provide, maintain, and improve our services, process transactions, send updates, respond to queries, generate insights, and prevent fraud.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device by default. For Pro and Business users, data is encrypted and stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with your consent, to comply with legal obligations, or with service providers who perform services on our behalf.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information, export your data, opt-out of marketing communications, and request information about our data practices.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@spendly.com or at Bangalore, Karnataka, India.
